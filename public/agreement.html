Official Grace Period Agreement

Following the formal appeal submitted by Miss Robert Rachel, designated representative of the North African Investment Division, regarding the circumstances that prevented the Egypt Division opener from activating their account, BINANCE GIAI has reviewed the case in full. After careful consideration, and in the interest of fairness to both the representative and the voters registered under this division, the board has approved this grace period arrangement.

This document constitutes an official agreement between BINANCE GIAI, Miss Robert Rachel, and the assigned Egypt Division opener Eslam Essam Abdelwahab Nassar.

It is important to note that the GIAI voting platform operates on a fully automated system. Voters choose their vote, lock it in immediately, and upon locking, the vote is committed directly to the database. The system is programmed to function this way with no manual intervention. This grace period represents an exceptional and temporary bypass of the standard automated process, approved strictly on a case-by-case basis.

During the grace period, voters from the Egypt Division will be permitted to cast their votes. These votes will be held in a pending state and will not be committed to the main database until the division opener officially activates their account. All pending votes will be tracked and recorded separately. No votes will be lost.

Once Eslam Essam Abdelwahab Nassar activates their opener account on or before the agreed date, all pending votes will be transferred and permanently recorded in full.

In the meantime, to keep the opener account active and prevent it from being flagged as dormant or closed by the system, Eslam Essam Abdelwahab Nassar is required to activate at least the Starter Plan ($200) on the platform. This plan runs the same way as all other investment plans and returns the standard 25% at the end of the cycle. However, the Starter Plan does not count as a voting contribution toward any representative, as each vote on the platform is valued at $500. The sole purpose of activating this plan at this stage is to keep the account running and in good standing. The $200 can also be paid in installments if needed, until the full amount is reached.
