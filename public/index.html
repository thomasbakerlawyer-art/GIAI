Competition Rules & Guidelines

Verified Participation

All investor activity and onboarding requests are monitored through GIAI AI verification systems. Only deposits processed through official wallet addresses and confirmed by our team count toward representative rankings during the active competition season.

Representative Rankings

Rankings increase based on verified investor onboarding and approved deposit activity. Every $500 invested under a representative awards them one vote. Rankings are updated dynamically throughout the season and displayed live on the homepage.

Security & Compliance

All representatives and investors must operate within GIAI global compliance policies and regional financial guidelines. Accounts flagged for irregular activity are suspended pending AI verification review.

Live Competition Tracking

Voting statistics, onboarding activity, and investment growth are updated dynamically throughout the competition period. The current season ranking resets at the start of each new GIAI cycle.
