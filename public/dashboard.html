What is the GIAI Gold Card?

Spending limit between $50,000 and $80,000, backed by your investment balance. Spend without needing cash upfront, and pay back over time within the agreed terms. The more you invest, the higher your limit can grow.

What is the GIAI Black Card?

GIAI's top tier: spending limit between $200,000 and $500,000. Reserved for investors on Capital Boost or Rapid Return plans with a balance of at least $30,000, which acts as collateral for this higher limit.
